Our story

Example Bakery began in 1998 in a family kitchen, with a jar of sourdough starter that is still alive today. What started as bread for neighbours grew into a small shop on Market Street, and later into the bakery you know now, with six bakers and a stone oven.

How we work

We bake in small batches, ferment our doughs slowly and buy flour from mills within a hundred kilometres. Leftover bread goes to the Springfield food bank every evening, so nothing we bake is wasted. We believe good bread takes time, and we are happy to take it.
