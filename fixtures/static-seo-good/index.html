Fresh sourdough bread, baked every morning

What we bake

Our bakers start before sunrise so that every loaf of sourdough, every croissant and every cinnamon roll on our shelves is fresh. We use stone-ground flour from local mills, filtered water and sea salt – nothing else. Our sourdough is fermented for thirty-six hours, which gives it a deep flavour, an open crumb and a crisp, blistered crust.

Visit the shop

You will find us at 12 Market Street in Springfield, open Tuesday to Sunday from seven in the morning until two in the afternoon. Orders for celebration cakes can be placed online up to three days in advance, and we are always happy to talk about allergies and ingredients.
