How to make a sourdough starter in 7 days

A sourdough starter is a living culture of wild yeast and bacteria. You only need flour, water, a jar and a little patience. This guide walks you through each of the seven days.

Day 1: mix flour and water

Mix fifty grams of wholemeal flour with fifty grams of lukewarm water in a clean jar. Cover it loosely and leave it somewhere warm, around twenty-four degrees Celsius.

Days 2 to 6: feed it every day

Each day, discard half of the starter and feed it with fresh flour and water in equal weights. Bubbles will appear, the smell will turn pleasantly sour and the starter will begin to rise and fall in a predictable rhythm.

Day 7: is it ready?

A ready starter doubles in size within six hours of feeding. Drop a spoonful into water: if it floats, it is ready to bake with. Keep it in the fridge between bakes and feed it once a week.
